# Keen Worlds implementation notes

This branch (`keen-worlds`) extends Omnispeak into one connected Commander
Keen 4–6 game with habitable, completed levels. The project's goals and
decisions are in `../concept.md` (project root); this file describes how the
engine side works. Keen Worlds code lives mainly in `src/kw_*.c`, and changes
to original Omnispeak files are marked `KEEN WORLDS`.

| File | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `src/kw_world.c` | Switching worlds, the Megarocket, Keen 5 gate, saves, `/KWTEST` |
| `src/kw_habitat.c` | Peaceful levels: residents, conversations, level edits and art, revisits |
| `src/kw_art.c` | Placing new art into maps and drawing it on cards |
| `src/kw_card.c` | The Keen Worlds card printed when the game quits |
| `src/kw_*_art.h`, `src/kw_barmaid_portrait.h` | Generated data — do not edit by hand |

Art and map data are generated by Python scripts in the project root's
`tools/` folder (outside this repo); see "New art" below.

## Running and testing

- `play/run.sh [4|5|6]` (project root) rebuilds Omnispeak, copies the binary
  to `play/` and starts the given world (default Keen 4). It prints the
  commit it runs and refuses to start if the build fails.
- `omnispeak /KWTEST` is the self-test. It runs without a display (build with
  `RENDERER=null`) and is best run under AddressSanitizer/UBSan. It covers:
  world switching between all installed worlds (Keen 7 when present),
  score travelling with Keen, story items staying in their world, Keen 7
  free of Keen 4/5 additions and all its levels loading, every sound effect
  after a switch, ship arrivals, the Keen 5 gate (placed, walkable,
  animated), endings, saves (cross-world and same-world), Border Village
  before and after liberation (enemies, residents, art, tavern doors, the
  fourth house's door), the revisit start and both exits, every level of
  every world when completed (entered at its exit, standing, facing in, gem
  doors open, leaving where Keen first came in), dialogue fitting
  its cards, art surviving memory purges, and loading every Keen 4 level.
- Known, harmless UBSan report: loading Keen 6 level 17 trips an
  out-of-bounds index in `RF_MarkTileGraphics` — the original engine's
  uninitialised `i`, documented in `id_rf.c`; it is never written through.

## The world registry

A **world** is a game Keen can fly to: one of the original episodes or a fan
game built on one of them. `kw_worldDefs[]` in `src/kw_world.c` lists them:

| Id | Menu name | Engine episode | Data folder | Variable file |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `keen4` | Gnosticus IV | 4 | (game path) | `EPISODE.CK4` |
| `keen5` | The Omegamatic | 5 | (game path) | `EPISODE.CK5` |
| `keen6` | Fribbulus Xax | 6 | (game path) | `EPISODE.CK6` |
| `keen7` | Krodacia | 4 | `mods/keen7` | `MOD.CK4` |
| `keen8` | Calidune | 6 | `mods/keen8` | `MOD.CK6` (ours) |
| `keen9` | HUB Command Center | 5 | `mods/keen9` | `MOD.CK5` (ours) |
| `osi` | The Shikadi Station | 5 (its own code) | `mods/osi` | its own `EPISODE.CK5` |
| `keen95` | Aspermontis | 4 | `mods/keen95` | `MOD.CK4` (written by `tools/kw_accumulators.py`) |
| `bounty` | Meurglys III | 4 | `mods/bounty` | `MOD.CK4` (written by `tools/kw_bounty.py`) |
| `ck55` | The Omegamatic II | 5 | `mods/ck55` | `ck55.ck5` (from the modding kit) |
| `asylum` | Address Unknown | 5 | `mods/asylum` | `ASYLUM.CK5` (from the modding kit) |

- **Installed or not**: a fan game is installed when its folder holds its
  variable file (`KW_IsWorldPresent`). The Megarocket menu lists only
  installed worlds; the W + 4…9 debug keys ignore missing ones.
- **Its files**: while a fan-game world is loaded its folder is searched
  first (`fs_worldPath` in `id_fs.c`), then the game path — so the mod's
  levels, graphics, sounds, headers and variable files replace the base
  episode's, exactly as if they had been copied over it.
- **Keen Worlds additions belong to a world**, not to an engine episode:
  the Keen 5 gate, the Keen 4/6 ship levels, Border Village's residents,
  edits, art and revisits are keyed by `KW_World`. Keen 7 runs on Keen 4's
  code but gets none of Keen 4's additions.
- **Per-world records**: map position, levels done, ending seen, and the
  story items (wetsuit, elders rescued, keycard, fuses, sandwich, rope,
  passcard, rocket). Items belong to their world because worlds on the same
  engine episode share the item fields (Keen 7 counts its own 7 elders).
  Score, lives and ammo travel with Keen.
- **Ships**: `kw_ships[]` lists worlds whose Megarocket stands in one of
  their levels (level, arrival spot at the ship's nose, boarding area):
  Keen 4 (level 18), Keen 6 (17), Keen 7 (18 — Keen 4's ship, three rows
  lower) and Keen 9 (level 1, the HUB Command Center: its ship stands on a
  platform at the level's right end, where the level starts Keen anyway).
  Keen 5 boards at its docked ship on the map. Keen 8 has a **Megarocket
  parked on its world map** (`kw_mapShips[]`, solid): Keen 4's own
  world-map Megarocket (tiles 1012-1013 of the Keen 4 shareware data,
  copied by `tools/kw_mapship_art.py` into tile slots no Keen 8 level uses —
  the mod has no empty slots left), on the sand a few steps below the
  map's own start. Keen lands right beside its nose — on arrival and when
  a game starts there (`KW_PlaceMapKeenAtShip`) — and boards there. A world with none of these
  boards and arrives at its world map's starting spot.
- **Starting in a world**: `/WORLD <id>` starts new games there
  (`play/run.sh 7` = `/EPISODE 4 /WORLD keen7`, `play/run.sh 8` =
  `/EPISODE 6 /WORLD keen8`, `play/run.sh 9` = `/EPISODE 5 /WORLD keen9`).

### Who made each world (credits)

Each `kw_worldDefs[]` row also carries the game's title, a byline, its
credits (pages separated by `\f`; fan games' are taken from their own Read
Me files) and a "how to get it" text.

- **Arrival card**: the first time Keen is on a world's map or at its ship
  (new game included) the game's title and makers are shown once
  (`KW_ShowArrivalCard`; not for a world a loaded save was made in, not in
  demos).
- **About this world**: the last line of the Megarocket menu shows the
  title card and the full credits.
- **Not installed**: the menu lists every world; choosing one that isn't
  installed shows what it is and which folder its files go in.
- `/KWTEST` checks every card fits the screen (`KW_CheckCardsFit`) and
  switches the cards off for itself.

- **Credits on arrival**: the first time Keen steps out of the ship onto
  a world, the arrival card goes on through the game's full credits (the
  same cards as "About this world" at the ship's controls). The small
  plaque that stood by the ship in every world is gone.

**Names**: a world is called by the name of the place in its own story,
never by its game's title: Gnosticus IV, the Omegamatic, Fribbulus Xax,
Krodacia, Calidune, the HUB Command Center (the space station Keen 9's
story lands him on; that game spans several planets from there), Krisle,
and Gnosticus Pi (Foray in the Forest writes it with the Greek letter, a
character of its own font).

### Keen 8: Dead in the Desert (converted)

Ceilick's Keen 6 mod (2009, patching by Levellass) was never converted for
Omnispeak, so `play/mods/keen8/MOD.CK6` is ours. The player puts the mod's
own files in `play/mods/keen8/` (`EGAGRAPH.CK6`, `EGAHEAD.CK6`, `AUDIO.CK6`,
`AUDIOHED.CK6`, `AUDIODCT.CK6`, `editmaps.ck6`, `maphead.ck6`, `Keen6.tli`,
`story.ck6`, `PAT.PAT`) plus Keen 6 **v1.4**'s `EGADICT.CK6`, `GFXINFOE.CK6`
and `AUDINFOE.CK6` from `omnispeak/data/keen6e14` (the mod's graphics are
compressed with v1.4's dictionary). Never the archive's `KEEN6.EXE`.

Done: levels, graphics, tile properties, sound and music, and all texts
(level names, entry texts, story, item and character lines). All 19 maps
load and every completed level is peaceful (`/KWTEST`).

Creature behaviour (2026-10-04): every active patch in `PAT.PAT` is
converted, as settings in `MOD.CK6` (each line names the patch address it
comes from). Nothing is keyed to the `keen8` world in C: the engine got
settings whose defaults are Keen 6's behaviour, so Keen 6 is unchanged.

- **Existing settings**: music per level, story and ending music, the
  creature question off, boss (Nospike) and Fleex health, the Gik's jump.
- **New settings** (`KEEN WORLDS` in `ck6_obj*.c`): `CK6_OrbatrixObjType`
  (Keen 8's is an item: touching it gives the red key),
  `CK6_OrbatrixAvoidsKeen`, `CK6_BloogguardClubAnyHeight`,
  `CK6_GikJumpKeenXRadiusMaxLeft`, `CK6_FlectKillsKeen`,
  `CK6_GrabbiterTalkPic`, `CK6_SatelliteZLayer`.
- **Redirected actions**: where a patch changes *which action the code
  goes to*, the code now asks `CK_ACTION_ALIAS(alias, original)`; a variable
  file redirects it with `%action alias = other` (after its `%override`).
  Used for `CK6_ACT_NospikeLanded`, `CK6_ACT_OrbatrixNearKeen`,
  `CK6_ACT_BobbaShootStart`, `CK6_ACT_BabobbaNapStart`.
- **Actions**: 35 changed and 2 new ones (Bobba's extra shooting frames),
  listed after `%override` in `MOD.CK6` because the original's action file
  would otherwise overwrite them.
- **New function** `CK6_WinGameCol`: the mod rewrites Keen 6's Ceilick
  collision into "win the game" and gives it to the stunned boss.

Kept as the mod has them, oddities included: one Grabbiter patch misses its
target by a byte and sets a speed instead of a timer; the patch that makes a
jumping Gik deadly does it by running the Orbatrix's rising code; a
collision function used as a think function (does nothing useful on DOS) is
NULL here. Not converted: the DOS start-up text banner (`CK6LOAD.BIN`),
which Keen Worlds never shows, and the patches the mod itself has commented
out.

`/KWTEST` checks the settings and redirects are in force in Keen 8, that
Keen 6 has none of them, that touching the stunned boss wins, and that the
Bobbas' borrowed sprites are in memory in every level that has one. Not yet
play-tested by eye.

### Converting a CKPatch file (`tools/kw_patconv.py`)

`python3 tools/kw_patconv.py all KEEN6.EXE PAT.PAT omnispeak/data/keen6e14`
reads the mod's patch file against the unmodified v1.4 program (fan games
ship it; it is only read) and prints: the changed and new actions as
ready `%action` lines; every code patch with the function it is in and the
old and new bytes (these need a setting or C by hand); other data patches.
`check ... MOD.CK6 CK6_WinGameCol=CK6_CeilickCol` then compares the finished
variable file with the patched program: all 331 Keen 8 and 332 Keen 9
actions match. `NAME=SEG:OFF` names code the patches add. The tool finds
the program's data segment itself and unpacks LZEXE programs.

### Keen 9: Battle of the Brains (converted)

Ceilick's Keen 5 mod (2011, patching by Levellass and Lemm):
`play/mods/keen9/` holds the mod's files (`EGAGRAPH.CK5`, `EGAHEAD.CK5`,
`AUDIO.CK5`, `AUDIOHED.CK5`, `AUDIODCT.CK5`, `editmaps.ck5`, `maphead.ck5`,
`Keen9.tli`, `story.ck5`, `PATCH5.PAT`) plus Keen 5 v1.4's `EGADICT.CK5`,
`GFXINFOE.CK5`, `AUDINFOE.CK5` from `omnispeak/data/keen5`, and our
`MOD.CK5`. All 18 maps load and are peaceful once completed.

Behaviour (2026-10-04): `PATCH5.PAT` is converted, the same way as Keen 8.

- **Settings** in `MOD.CK5`, each naming its patch address: about 60 new
  ones in `ck5_obj2.c`, `ck5_obj3.c`, `ck5_misc.c`, `ck5_map.c`,
  `ck_keen.c`, `ck_obj.c`, `ck_play.c`, `ck_text.c` and `id_ca.c` (all
  `KEEN WORLDS`, defaults are Keen 5's behaviour), plus existing ones.
- **12 redirected actions** (`CK_ACTION_ALIAS`), e.g. Robo Red's shooting
  goes to the Shikadi's pole zap, the Master's to Robo Red's shots.
- **196 actions** after `%override`, 20 of them new.
- **New program code** in the patch file is `src/kw_keen9.c` (`KW9_...`):
  the friends' messages where Keen 5 has fuses (Spot, Lindsey, Barker, the
  Janitor, by level), the Warpcore in level 15 (hit by enemy shots, or set
  off by touch), the yeti (three shots), snowballs that knock Keen over,
  the Korathoid (where Keen 5 has standing platforms), a mine that explodes
  when shot, a Sphereful that can be stunned.
- **Music**: Keen 9 has 19 songs starting at audio chunk 187; `MOD.CK5`
  sets `ca_audStartMusic` and `ca_audNumSongs` (new), so the level music is
  right now (it was five songs off before).
- **Sprite caches**: the mod moves every creature's frames; `lumpStarts` and
  `lumpEnds` in `MOD.CK5` follow. Omnispeak keeps that table sorted by
  sprite number, the program doesn't, so they are matched by start value.

Not converted: the DOS start-up banner, the galaxy-explosion palette and
star colours (Omnispeak's explosion is hard-wired), the removed F10 cheats.
Found on the way: Omnispeak's Keen 5 action file names two different
program functions `CK_DeadlyCol`; one only kills (`CK_LethalCol`). Keen 9's
file uses the right one; Keen 5's own data is left as upstream has it.

`/KWTEST` checks Keen 9's settings, redirects and new functions are wired
up, that Keen 5 has none of them, and, for every level of Keen 8 and 9,
that each creature's sprites are in memory. Not yet play-tested by eye.

### Atroxian Realm (in progress)

A game of its own (Gridlock, 2016, GPL source written from Omnispeak's Keen
5 code), world id `arealm`. `src/kw_arealm.c` has its episode definition
(`EP_CK5` with extension `CKD`) and its level setup; its variable files are

list after every finished level, as its option does.

### The Megarocket (Keen Worlds' own world)

World id `megarocket`: the inside of Keen's ship, on Keen 4's engine. Its
map is written by `tools/kw_shipworld.py` into `data/megarocket`
(`play/run.sh` copies the folder to `play/megarocket`).

Its look is the ship of **Foray in the Forest** (Nisaba and K1n9_Duk3, GPL
v2 or later): that game's flight room and exit room, with the things lying
about in them painted out. `tools/kw_shiproom_art.py` reads the two rooms
from the game's own files (`play/mods/fitf`, level 1), cleans them and
writes `src/kw_shiproom_art.h`, which is placed like the other Keen Worlds
art. So Foray's art is in this repository; the world's card and "About
this world" name its makers. (The user is to speak to the authors about
this use before anything is published.)

Keen Worlds adds three things of Keen's to the exit room (own drawing):
his pogo stick, his bag and a bed (the way into Keen Dreams).

The flight room follows Foray's own layout (read from its level and tile
properties): Keen can jump up onto the flight deck, which is as wide as
Foray's so he stays under the glass, and the box of stunners under the deck
is the ammo station. "Up" opens the
list of worlds at the navigation screen in the nose, where a small sign
with an arrow blinks, and also on the flight deck at the controls.

**Every game begins inside the ship** (`KW_BeginInShip`, called for a new
game): the ship has landed on the world the game starts in, and Keen steps
out through the hatch. Choosing a world at the controls no longer puts Keen
outside: the screen goes dark, the ship now stands on that world, and Keen
is still inside. On Krisle the controls then refuse (the ship is wrecked
until that game is finished).

**Landing announcements**: when the ship has landed (new game, or after a
flight) the ship computer says where, with a warning about the locals: the
same woman's voice for every world. `tools/kw_landing_voice.py` holds the
words; it writes the clips (`data/voice/LAND_<ID>.RAW`) and `src/kw_landing_text.h` for the card shown with them. The voice is
Kokoro's "Heart" (a free neural voice run locally through the kokoro-js
copy and model in the user's ArcadiumCMS project, `tools/kw_landing_voice.mjs`).
The engine plays one voice clip at a time, mixed into the SDL sound output
(`SD_PlayVoice` in `id_sd.c`). The ship's tune is silent while she speaks:
it fades out with the lights before a landing and fades back in afterwards
(`SD_FadeAdLibVolume`).

**Music**: Keen 9 has 19 songs starting at audio chunk 187; `MOD.CK5`
  sets `ca_audStartMusic` and `ca_audNumSongs` (new), so the level music is
  right now (it was five songs off before).
- **Sprite caches**: the mod moves every creature's frames; `lumpStarts` and
  `lumpEnds` in `MOD.CK5` follow. Omnispeak keeps that table sorted by
  sprite number, the program doesn't, so they are matched by start value.

Not converted: the DOS start-up banner, the galaxy-explosion palette and
star colours (Omnispeak's explosion is hard-wired), the removed F10 cheats.
Found on the way: Omnispeak's Keen 5 action file names two different
program functions `CK_DeadlyCol`; one only kills (`CK_LethalCol`). Keen 9's
file uses the right one; Keen 5's own data is left as upstream has it.

`/KWTEST` checks Keen 9's settings, redirects and new functions are wired
up, that Keen 5 has none of them, and, for every level of Keen 8 and 9,
that each creature's sprites are in memory. Not yet play-tested by eye.

### The Megarocket (Keen Worlds' own world)

v2 or later): that game's flight room and exit room, with the things lying

this use before anything is published.)

Keen Worlds adds three things of Keen's to the exit room (own drawing):

The flight room follows Foray's own layout (read from its level and tile
properties): Keen can jump up onto the flight deck, which is as wide as
Foray's so he stays under the glass, and the box of stunners under the deck
is the ammo station. "Up" opens the
list of worlds at the navigation screen in the nose, where a small sign
with an arrow blinks, and also on the flight deck at the controls.

**Every game begins inside the ship** (`KW_BeginInShip`, called for a new
game): the ship has landed on the world the game starts in, and Keen steps

**Landing announcements**: when the ship has landed (new game, or after a
flight) the ship computer says where, with a warning about the locals, in a
different voice for every world. `tools/kw_landing_voice.py` holds the
words; it writes the clips (`data/megarocket/LAND_<ID>.RAW`, made with
espeak-ng and ffmpeg) and `src/kw_landing_text.h` for the card shown with
them. The engine plays one voice clip at a time, mixed into the SDL sound
output (`SD_PlayVoice` in `id_sd.c`).

**Voices** (`data/voice`, copied to `play/voice`; 16-bit mono, 22050 Hz):
`KW_Speak` in `kw_music.c` plays a clip. Music never plays under a voice:
it fades out, stays off while the clip plays, and fades back in when the
clip is over (the ship's tune, the tavern's, or the level's song). Use it
for every voice that is added.

**The barmaid's talk** (`tools/kw_barmaid_voice.py` ->
`src/kw_barmaid_talk.h` and the clips `BAR_nn.RAW`): a conversation of
several places, written once in the tool. At each she says something and
Keen picks one of up to four lines; she answers and the talk goes on or
ends. She knows the worlds and who made each, and GriNoir the great Linker
on her wall. All her lines are spoken (Kokoro's "Bella"). `/KWTEST` checks
that every card fits and every clip is there.

A voice stops when its card is put away. At the tavern's door the music
outside fades down and the tavern's tune comes up, and the other way round
(`KW_UpdateMusicZone`).

**Each world's own story**: when a world is chosen, that game's story
scrolls away into the distance as in its title sequence (any key skips it;
once a game for each world Keen has not been to):
- a new game: before the ship (`KW_BeginInShip`);
- at the ship's controls: during the flight. The game loop loads the chosen
  world's pictures and letters, plays the story, and loads the ship again
  (`KW_StoryBeforeLanding`); Keen is by the hatch when it has landed, and
  the computer announces the landing.
- A world reached some other way shows it when Keen first sets foot there
  (`KW_ShowWorldStory`).

Foray in the Forest's and Atroxian Realm's story texts are written into
their variable files by `tools/kw_fitf.py` (from the game's text chunk; its
font is in ASCII order: `ck_starWarsPlainText`) and `tools/kw_arealm.py`
(from its source); before, both showed Keen 5's. `KW_AUTOSTORY=1` plays
the stories in scripted developer runs.

**Two ways to play** (asked when a game begins, kept in its saved games):
- **Free Flight**: every installed world is open, in any order.
- **The Chronicle**: the worlds of the story open in its order
  (`kw_chronicleLine`: Keen 4 to Keen 9, then The Eight Accumulators, each
  opened by saving the one before). Worlds off that line are bound to one
  world of it or to none (`KW_ChronicleKey`): the Shikadi Station, the
  Omegamatic II and Address Unknown open when the Omegamatic is saved;
  Krisle, Gnosticus Pi and Meurglys III are open from the start. A game
  begins on the first world of the line whichever game was launched. The
  flight menu shows locked worlds in grey and says
  which world must be saved first. When a world has been saved, the ship
  computer announces the new course the next time Keen is in the cabin
  (`COURSE_<ID>.RAW`). Worlds that are not installed are passed over; a
  world Keen has been to stays open. `KW_AUTOCHRONICLE=1` chooses it in
  scripted developer runs. It is kept in the save block's spare words.

**Keen Worlds' own title, story and ending.** The opening shows Keen
Worlds' title picture instead of the running episode's (`KW_DrawTitlePicture`;
the episode's "Commander Keen" scroller, which ends on the episode's title,
is left out unless `/KWTERMINATOR` is given), and scrolls Keen Worlds' own
story (`kw_ownStory`, `KW_PlayOwnStory`) over the episode's star field.
Each world's own story still plays when that world is chosen. When every installed world is saved, in either way of playing, the
ending picture is shown once, in the cabin. The story scrolls over a picture of its own (`KW_DrawStoryBackdrop`), in
fifteen colours picked for it (the sixteenth is the letters'); the scroller

The opening has no demos (`/KWDEMOS` brings them back): title, story, high

`KW_AUTOTITLE=file.ppm` saves
the title (with `KW_AUTOENDING=1` the ending, `=story` the story's picture) and quits;
`KW_AUTOOWNSTORY=1` plays the story in a scripted run.

**The title tune**, "The Greatest Adventure" (`kw_titleMusic`, written by
`tools/kw_title_music.py`): just under a minute of funk in A Dorian for the
OPL2, in the sounds of the Megarocket's tune played by a bigger band: a
syncopated bass, three-note horn stabs, a lead that slides into some notes,
and the chip's drums with tom fills and a cymbal. `KW_StartTitleMusic`
starts it with the title picture when the program is launched; after eight
seconds the story scrolls, the tune playing on, and then the demos begin.
`tools/kw_imf2wav.c` renders a tune to a WAV file with the engine's own chip
emulator, to hear it without the game. When a game begins from the menu,
the title shown is the game's own (Keen 4's for Keen 4), then its story.

**Keen 4 speaks.** Every line of dialogue in Secret of the Oracle has a
voice clip (`data/voice/K4_*.RAW`, made by `tools/kw_keen4_voice.py` with
Kokoro): Keen (bm_fable, his voice in every world from now on), the council
members and the village elder (bm_george; the one rescued under water is

Princess Lindsey (bf_emma), the janitor (am_eric) and the ending's

window appears (`ck4_misc.c`; the ending page by page in `ck_text.c`,
`K4_END_<page>.RAW`), with the music turned down under it, not stopped, and
brought back up afterwards (`KW_LinesOver`, `KW_LinePoll`). Only in the
world `keen4`: the fan games on the same engine have other words in the
same windows. `KW_AUTOTALK=n` runs all the dialogues at frame n of a
scripted run and prints each clip.

Keen's voice says the word "Keen" near "Cain" by itself, so its phonemes
are pushed towards "kyeen" (`STYLE` in `tools/kw_keen4_voice.py`;
`tools/kw_voice.mjs` can replace phonemes). Both voices were picked by ear
from samples. In the
conversations with the elder and the barmaid, the line Keen picks stands on
a card of his own and he says it (`KW_KeenSays`; clips `KEENSAY_*.RAW`,
named after the line's text).

**Keen 5 and Keen 6 speak** too (`K5_*.RAW`, `K6_*.RAW`, made by
`tools/kw_keen56_voice.py`). Keen 5 is Keen at a broken fuse and a narrated
ending (its secret ending has two pages of its own, `K5S_END_*`). Keen 6
writes like a storybook, so a clip is spoken in turns and joined: Keen, the
narrator, the Grabbiter (am_fenrir), the Bloogstar's door (am_adam, made a
machine's) and Molly McMire (af_jessica). The endings' last, selling pages
are silent. A line still being spoken when play goes on is cut
(`KW_UpdateMusicZone`).

**The flight menu** (`KW_TravelMenu`): the worlds in two columns of two
groups each (Goodbye, Galaxy!; The Universe Is Toast!; Other adventures;
More levels), left and right between the columns. A line under the list
names the game the chosen world is and how things stand with it. The world
the ship stands on is in the list; choosing it tells about it.
`KW_AUTOMENU=file.ppm` saves a picture of the menu in scripted runs.

**About Keen Worlds** (`KW_ShowAboutKeenWorlds`): whoever put this galaxy
together writes their own words in `data/megarocket/ABOUT.TXT` (plain text;
a line of three dashes starts a new card; a line up to 34 letters, a card up
to 16 lines). The flight menu has the line "About Keen Worlds" under its
left column, and the cards are shown once more after the ending picture.

**Gnosticus Pi's arrival**: flown to for the first time from the ship's
controls, its story plays, then the game's own fly-in over its map, and
when the ship has landed Keen is still inside it (`KW_FitfShipLanded`): the
computer announces the landing and the hatch is the way out, to beside the
landed ship in its level. The door of that landed ship leads into the
Megarocket's cabin, not into the game's own copy of the ship's rooms.

**A world saved**: when a world's game has been won and Keen is next in the
cabin, the ship computer says its piece about it, once (`SAVED_<ID>.RAW`,
words in `tools/kw_landing_voice.py`), in both ways of playing. The flight
menu shows such worlds in green. In the Chronicle a landing on a new world
is headed by a chapter card (the worlds counted in the order Keen comes to
them), and when every installed world is saved a last card says so. All
this is kept in the save block's spare words (with what dying takes back on
the Shikadi Station).

**After dying**: the engine's menu has a third choice in every world when
there is a saved game to go back to: "Load last save" (the slot last saved
to or loaded from; `US_LoadQuickSave`). Foray in the Forest has this in its
own menu.

**Krisle's crash**: the first flight to Krisle (after the one-way warning)
does not land. Its story plays, then the level of the crash site comes up
under flashes and bangs (`KW_CrashLanding`), the ship computer makes its
last announcement, and Keen is outside by the wreck, stranded until that
world is saved. A new game started on Krisle begins the same way, without
the cabin. Once Krisle is saved the ship lands there like anywhere else.

**Foray's landed ship**: going in through its door (which the game answers
by putting Keen in its own copy of the ship's rooms, lower in the same
level) boards the Megarocket instead, where the controls are.

**Music**: inside the ship an original space-funk tune plays
(`tools/kw_ship_music.py` -> `src/kw_ship_music.h`, started by
`KW_StartShipMusic`). It uses the OPL2's drum mode, which the sound
emulator (`opl/dbopl.c`) aborted on; that is fixed there.

The box of stunners gives Keen 20 shots whenever he has fewer.

Around the ship the screen is black, as in Foray. (The picture is
see-through there and the level's background tiles are black.)

The two rooms are one level, each room a screen, one above the other with
a Keen 4 scroll stop between them. Walking into the open end of a room puts
Keen in the other (`KW_ShipChangeRoom` in `kw_world.c`).

Boarding a ship in any world takes Keen into the exit room. "Up" at the hatch goes back out to the world the ship stands on (`KW_ShipRoomFrame`;
the map marks all these places in its info plane). Without the folder the
menu opens at the ship as before. It is not a destination itself. The save
block remembers which world the ship stands on.

### Keen Dreams (the bed)

World id `dreams`, "Tuberia": Keen Dreams (id Software, 1991; GPL source
since 2014), running in this engine. It is not a planet. It is the dream
Keen has in the bed of the Megarocket's exit room (`KW_Dream` in
`kw_world.c`; the map marks the bed in its info plane): the flight console
doesn't list it, the ship computer says nothing about it, the Chronicle
leaves it out (`KW_IsPlanet`), and nothing is carried in or out. Keen's
score, lives and shots wait in the ship while he dreams (`KW_Tally`,
`KW_DreamLeaving`, `KW_DreamArriving`); the dream keeps its own score,
lives, flower power, bombs and keys, in the save block's spare words from
170.

Keen Dreams is the engine Keen 4 to 6 grew out of, so it runs as Keen 4
with its own code on top:

- `tools/kw_dreams.py` converts the player's copy of the game (shareware
  1.13: the tables inside `KDREAMS.EXE`, the Huffman-packed maps) into the
  engine's file layout, in `play/mods/dreams/*.KDR`.
- `tools/kw_dreams_port.py` writes its sprite states as an action file
  (`KDACTION.KDR`), the world's variable file (`DREAMS.KDR`, on Keen 4's)
  and `src/kw_dreams_defs.h` from the Keen Dreams source, and makes the
  first pass over `KD_KEEN.C`, `KD_ACT1.C` and `KD_ACT2.C`: the old names
  to Omnispeak's (`objtype` to `CK_object`, `ob->xmove` while thinking to
  `ck_nextX`, `tinf[NORTHWALL+tile]` to `TI_ForeTop`, ...). The results,
  finished by hand, are `src/kw_kd_keen.c` (Keen, flower power, the map
  Keen), `kw_kd_act1.c` and `kw_kd_act2.c` (the vegetables and Boobus
  Tuber).
- `src/kw_dreams.c` is what its `KD_PLAY.C` did around them: the info plane
  (`KD_ScanInfoLayer`), its scrolling, which is also how a level ends, by
  walking off the map's edge (`KD_ScrollScreen`), the map of Tuberia
  (`KD_PatchWorldMap`), the two-button input (`KD_FrameStart`: both of the
  engine's other buttons throw), dying (keys and the level's bombs go) and
  the ending in its own words (`KD_GameFinale`).

The engine's wall clipping, state machine and score box are used as they
are (they descend from Keen Dreams'); hooks marked `KD_IsActive()` in
`ck_play.c`, `ck_game.c` and `ck_keen.c` switch off what Keen 4 added
(tile checks, platforms carrying Keen, the countdown box, music).

Keen has no wrist computer in his pyjamas: in the dream the menu keys ask
whether to wake up (`KW_DreamKeys`), and Enter shows a plain status window.
The bed on the map of Tuberia is a way back too, as the ship is on other
worlds: walking up into it from its foot asks the same question. Waking puts Keen back in his bunk with the dream kept as it is; games are
saved from the ship. Out of lives, he wakes with a start and the next dream
begins again (`KW_DreamOver`); so it does after the ending. Without the
converted files Keen "is not sleepy".

### Adding a fan game

1. Get it converted for Omnispeak (the Omnispeak modding kit includes Keen
   7, Keen Asylum and Keen 5.5; see `../research/mods-as-worlds.md`).
2. Add a `KW_World` value and a `kw_worldDefs[]` row (id, name, base
   episode, folder under the game path, its variable file).
3. Players put the converted folder at `play/mods/<id>/`.
4. Add it to `/KWTEST`'s route; check all its levels load.

Keen Worlds does not ship fan games: players install them, as they supply
Keen 4–6.

## Travelling between worlds

- `KW_SwitchWorld()` frees the current episode's graphics, sounds, maps and
  variables (`CA_UnloadEpisodeData`, `CK_VAR_Reset`) and loads another. It
  re-sets the sound mode afterwards (AdLib effects are located by the
  episode's sound count).
- Each world remembers Keen's map position, finished levels, whether its
  ending has been seen and its story items (see the registry above). Score,
  lives and ammo travel with Keen.
- The Bean-with-Bacon Megarocket is the way to travel:

  | World | Board at | Arrive at |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Keen 4 | the ship in its BWB Megarocket level (18) | that level, tile 3 at the ship's nose |
  | Keen 5 | the docked ship beside the map start (walk across the gate) | the Keen 5 map |
  | Keen 6 | the ship in its BWB Megarocket level (17) | that level, tile 4 at the ship's nose |
  | Keen 7 | the ship in its BWB Megarocket level (18) | that level, tile 3 at the ship's nose |

  Press jump, pogo or fire at the ship to open the destination menu. Arrivals
  stand at the ship's nose rather than under it: the hull at Keen's height is
  drawn in front of sprites (tile misc bit `0x80`), which would hide him.
- Debug shortcuts, on a world map or at the ship: hold **W** and press
  **4** to **9**, **K** (Krisle) or **F** (Gnosticus Pi) to fly to that
  world (this also leaves Krisle), or **E** to
  play the ending of the world Keen is in (`KW_DebugEnding`: the world then
  counts as completed, every level of it is marked completed, and Keen is put
  at his ship).

## Keen 5 boarding gate

New EGA art (`tools/kw_gate_art.py` → `src/kw_gate_art.h`): a gate with a
pulsing energy field, a ribbed tube with running lights and a collar on the
ship's nose, three animation frames, covering map tiles 29–31 × 94–96. It
replaces the solid gangway knob at (29–30, 95); Keen walks along the middle
row and is kept on it (`KW_ConstrainKeen5Dock`). The walkway cell where the
tube meets the ship's nose (31, 95) is solid (`KW_Art.isSolidCell`), so Keen
stops right beside the ship instead of walking onto it, and boards there
(`/KWTEST` walks him along it). The unused yellow map border
is replaced in memory with a starfield tile.

## Saves, settings, high scores, endings

- One set of save slots for all worlds: `KWORLDS0.SAV` … `KWORLDS9.SAV`.
  Slot headers carry the tag `KWS`, so every save is listed in every world.
  Original Omnispeak/DOS saves are not loaded.
- A save starts with a Keen Worlds block (`KW_WriteSaveBlock`: id `KWLD`,
  version 2, the id of the world it was saved in, then every world's record
  by id), then the normal Omnispeak level save. Records of worlds that are no
  longer installed are dropped; a save made in a world that isn't installed
  won't load. Version 1 saves (Keen 4–6 only) still load. Bump
  `KW_SAVE_VERSION` when the block changes.
- Loading a save from another world can't switch episodes while the menu is
  open; it finishes after the menu closes (`KW_FinishDeferredLoad`).
- One config file, `KWORLDS.CFG`: settings and a single high-score table.
- New games start in the world the program was launched in.
- After a world's original ending the run continues on that world's map; the
  world counts as completed. High scores are submitted at game over. The
  original ending pages are unchanged.
- When the game quits, the terminal shows the Keen Worlds card
  (`src/kw_card.c`: byline, title, body lines) instead of each episode's
  order screen.

## Habitable levels

**Every completed level, in every world**, can be entered again and is
peaceful:

**Border Village tavern music.** The reopened tavern in Keen 4's Border Village has its own tune: an original
jig in G major (squeezebox, plucked bass, off-beat chords) written by
`tools/kw_tavern_music.py` into `src/kw_tavern_music.h` as an OPL2/IMF loop on
AdLib channels 1-4 (channel 0 is the sound effects'), played by `src/kw_music.c`. It starts only while Keen is inside the
room carved below the inn (tiles x 64–82, y 26–34), and the level's normal
music resumes when he steps back outside. The loop is compiled into Keen
Worlds and played through `SD_StartMusicData`, so it does not modify or ship
the player's `AUDIO.CK4` archive. The zone is explicitly keyed to
`KW_KEEN4`, level 1, and only exists after the village has been liberated.

- **Entrance kept**: completed levels normally lose their world-map entrance
  (`CK_MapLevelMarkAsDone`); Keen Worlds keeps them all. Leaving a level that
  was already completed doesn't replay the flag-throwing animation.
- **Enemies removed** after the level spawns (`KW_IsHostile`, Keen 4, 5 and 6
  lists; Keen 4 dart guns are not handled yet).
- **Story moments don't replay**: Keen 5's QED, Keen 6's Molly and the
  rope, sandwich and passcard (Keen already has them) are removed; Keen 4's
  rescued elders stay as friendly villagers who can't be rescued again.
- **Walking back in from the other end** (`KW_PlaceRevisitStart`, found
  automatically for every level of every world): Keen starts where he
  first *finished* the level, facing back into it, so it looks like he
  walks back. In order:
  1. the exit door (Keen 5, info `0xB1B1`; Keen stands one row below the
     marker);
  2. a goal: Keen 5's fuses (shown broken) or where the QED stood, the
     Oracle Council member a Keen 4/7 level rescues, Keen 6's rope,
     sandwich, passcard or Molly — the nearest standing spot to it;
  3. otherwise the side exit farther from the level's start: the standing
     spot nearest the edge whose space runs out to the edge, or, if
     something is in the way (e.g. a pillar), the nearest spot to an
     opening in the edge column;
  4. a level whose only way out is by its start (Keen 6's Bloogfoods and
     Bloog Control before the goal rule, three Keen 7 levels) keeps that.
  Swimming levels (Keen 4's Well of Wishes) keep their own start, and so
  does a world's **Megarocket level** (an arrival by ship is placed beside
  the ship as always); it also has no "walk back out where you came in"
  exit, which would end it as soon as Keen arrives.
  `kw_revisitStarts[]` can override a level by hand (empty now).
- **Leaving**: the level's normal exits, walking into the first or last 3
  tiles of the map at either end, and walking back out where Keen first
  came in (his original start, in the direction he came from — needed where
  a level starts mid-map) (`KW_CheckPeacefulExit`).
- **Completely open**: gem doors are open (holders filled, the door
  opening as if the gem had been placed: `KW_OpenGemDoors`), Keen 5's
  keycard doors need no card, and deadly tiles (spikes, fire) don't hurt
  (`KW_IsDeadlyTileHarmless`, checked in `ck_keen.c`).
- **Residents** (talking characters) live in some levels only; see below.
- **Level changes**: tile edits (`KW_MapEdit`, when: always / after
  completion) and art (`kw_levelArt[]`, when: after — **the default for Keen
  Worlds art**, "the villagers are back with their things" — before, or
  always), applied by `KW_ChangeLevel()`.

### Residents and conversations

Residents are listed in `kw_residents[]` (`src/kw_habitat.c`): world, level,
where they stand, their greeting, up to three **choices** (what Keen can say
and their answer), and their kind:

- `KW_ELDER`: an Oracle Council elder spawned as a character (council
  sprites, spawned as `CT_Friendly` so he can't be "rescued" again; tagged in
  `user4`, which saves with the game). Picture: the game's `PIC_ORACLE`.
- `KW_PAINTED`: drawn into level art (the barmaid); a talk spot instead of a
  character. Picture: her own art (`tools/kw_barmaid_portrait.py`).

Stand next to a resident and press **up**. The resident greets Keen on a card
with their picture; Keen's card (his picture, the choices, a blinking
selection box like the Megarocket menu — up/down, Enter or jump; Esc backs
out) lets the player pick what he says; the resident answers on a third card.
Lines use `\n`; `/KWTEST` measures every line against the card size.

## New art (`src/kw_art.c`)

Generators in `tools/` draw art in the 16 EGA colours, `preview` it over the
real level and `csource` it into a header. `KW_PlaceArt()` lays each art tile
over the original tile beneath it, stores it in an empty masked-tile slot,
and sets its tile properties: collision and misc flags copied from the
original (none on a walkable row, misc cleared with `clearMisc`), animation
links between frames. Player data files are never changed.

- **Tile info layout** (foreground, see `id_ti.c`): 0 top, 1 right,
  2 bottom, 3 left, 4 animation offset, 5 misc, 6 animation time.
- **Slots**: a generator only uses slots that have no graphics in EGAGRAPH,
  appear on no level, and are not the target of an original animation link
  (Keen 4's tile 1275 links to the empty 1276). Each piece reserves its own
  slot range. Animated art keeps each tile's frames close together (links
  are int8 offsets).
- **Memory**: art chunks are locked — they exist only in memory and must
  never be purged (a dialogue's cache level made the memory manager purge
  them once). `MM_MAXBLOCKS` is raised from 2048 to 8192.
- **Drawing on cards**: `KW_BuildArtTiles` + `KW_DrawArt` (the engine's
  `VHB_DrawTile16M` draws an 8x8 tile by mistake).
- Generators: `kw_gate_art.py`, `kw_portrait_art.py` (the creator's portrait,
  from `assets/creator-portrait-source.jpg`), `kw_barhouse.py` (inn, tavern,
  sign, boards, furniture, bar), `kw_barmaid_portrait.py`, plus helpers
  `keenmap.py` (levels), `keenrender.py` (tiles, sprites) and `kw_bar_art.py`
  (canvas and pixel font).
