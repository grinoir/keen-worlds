# Where the games fit in time

Discussion draft, 2026-10-25. This proposes a reading/play order for Keen
Worlds' Chronicle. It preserves connected stories and uses authored placement
for independent adventures. It is not an official shared chronology for all
the original games and fan games, and contains no invented calendar dates.

## Agreed: original Keen 1–3 belong on the ship's arcade machine

If the original episodes are integrated, play them through **an arcade
machine in a corner of the Megarocket cabin**, as requested by the user.

The cabinet's game list follows the order of Keen's earlier adventures:

1. **Keen 1 — Marooned on Mars**
2. **Keen 2 — The Earth Explodes**
3. **Keen 3 — Keen Must Die!**

Their stories precede the main Chronicle, but playing them is optional and
the cabinet can be used whenever Keen is aboard the ship.

Proposed behavior:

- Walk up to the cabinet and press Up to choose an installed game.
- Return to the same cabin position when leaving it.
- Keep arcade progress and high scores separate from the main adventure.
  Arcade lives, ammunition and items do not transfer to Keen Worlds.
- Arcade completion does not unlock planets or count towards the
  all-worlds-saved ending.
- Keep the cabinet clear of the hatch, bed, console and cabin walking route.
- The local development build now uses a native Chocolate Keen companion
  for original Keen 1–3, with player-supplied v1.31 data. Complete human play
  checks remain. The Galaxy remake is also playable natively with its own
  saves under arcade/mars.

### Keen 1: choose the original or the Galaxy remake

**User decision, 2026-10-25:** Selecting Keen 1 on the cabinet offers the
original game or **Marooned on Mars — Galaxy**, rather than giving the remake
a separate destination or flashback access point.

Proposed Keen dialogue:

> Hey! There's an updated version of my first adventure with enhanced
> graphics! Which one should I play?

Show a clear choice:

- **Original Keen 1**
- **Galaxy remake — enhanced graphics**
- **Back**

Keep each version's saves/progress separate and identify the remake and its
author in the game information. Both versions tell the earlier Mars adventure;
neither adds a second present-day visit to the main Chronicle. Show the
comparison line when both supported versions are installed. If one is
missing, label it unavailable with the existing installation guidance rather
than suggesting it can already launch. The original games now have native runtime integration in the development
build; the Galaxy remake now runs natively too. Both spoken introductions
use Keen's established voice and follow the voice preferences.

### Duke Nukem arcade reference

**User request:** Include the Duke Nukem arcade quote. The remembered line
is **“Hmm, don't have time to play with myself.”**, said when using the
Duke Nukem II cabinet in Duke Nukem 3D's Hollywood Holocaust level.
[Reference and context](https://dukenukem.fandom.com/wiki/Hollywood_Holocaust).

Proposed staging: Keen says the quoted line when first using his own cabinet,
then adds **“Well… maybe one game!”** before the game list opens. The follow-up
is our proposed addition, not part of Duke's original quote. Keep the joke
clearly tied to playing a game starring himself. Use it as an occasional
interaction, not a repeated line every time Keen walks past. Text remains
available with voices off; any spoken version follows the voice settings.

## Proposed Chronicle order

The order of the larger blocks below is our proposal. Dependencies inside
connected story lines should be preserved.

| Stage | Games | Placement and reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Earlier adventure, optional arcade game | **Marooned on Mars — Galaxy** | A retelling of Keen 1, set before Keen 4. Select it as the enhanced-graphics alternative under Keen 1 on the ship's cabinet. It stays outside the main Chronicle. |
| 1. The established opening | **Keen 4 → Keen 5 → Keen 6** | Retain the current Keen Worlds Chronicle opening. This is our existing reading order, not a new assertion about disputed original-game dates. |
| 2. The Universe Is Toast | **The Keys of Krodacia → Dead in the Desert → Battle of the Brains** | Keep the trilogy together in its existing order. Avoid inserting unrelated adventures into an ongoing chase. |
| 3. The journey home | **The Eight Accumulators** | Keep the existing follow-up to Battle of the Brains. |
| 4. Back on Earth | **Suburb Shenanigans** | Place it after the universe-saving adventures and the return journey. Its story says Keen has recently saved the universe; the neighbourhood adventure makes a natural homecoming chapter. |
| 5. Return visits | **Return to the Shadowlands → Operation Station Infiltration** | Revisit Gnosticus IV, then continue the same adventure at the Shikadi station. The author identifies Station Infiltration as the second episode of this series. This sequence stands alongside the original Galaxy games rather than replacing Keen 6. |
| 6. The Atroxian story | **Atroxian Realm → The Alphamatic** | Answer the distress call on Krisle, then follow its sequel. Keep these adjacent; The Alphamatic continues the Atroxian story. |
| 7. A new expedition | **Operation Ocflore** | Proposed standalone expedition after the Atroxian arc. This is a pacing choice, not an author-established date. |
| Optional interlude | **Foray in the Forest** | A wider-adventures interlude at a natural break after the central journey. Its exact relationship to other fan stories needs a closer story-text review; do not invent a strict dependency. |
| 8. Craig's adventures | **Aurora Bliss → its epilogue → Canmore's Redemption** | Place this after another return home, then keep the connected adventures together. Check the original epilogue/puzzle conditions before deciding how Canmore unlocks. |
| 9. The bounty-hunter trilogy | **Bounty Hunting Brouhaha → The Prisoner's Dilemma → Send in the Clorts** | A later travelling-Keen arc, kept in its authors' order. Its position after the other independent arcs is our proposed finale placement. |
| Closing chapter | **Keen Worlds' own ending** | Follow the supported campaign's completion rules. Arcade games remain outside the required world-completion count. |

The relative order of stages 5–8 is flexible until the complete story/ending
texts have been checked. The draft gives us a usable order without turning
an unverified relationship into an official fact.

## Existing optional adventures

- **Keen 5.5** and **Keen Asylum** can remain optional adventures available
  after Keen 5, matching the current broad unlock rule. Recommend playing
  them at a break between story blocks; their numbering is not evidence of
  an exact date.
- **Keen Dreams** stays behind the ship's bed. It is a dream outside the
  numbered chapter chain and can be visited independently.
- **Free Flight** continues to let players choose installed destinations
  without following this proposed reading order.

## Confirmed story connections and evidence

- **Return to the Shadowlands → Operation Station Infiltration:**
  [Ceilick's release announcement](https://pckf.com/viewtopic.php?t=15448)
  describes Station Infiltration as episode two and points players to
  Return to the Shadowlands as episode one. The installed Station story
  concerns rescuing Keen's pet Yorp, a separate mission from rescuing Molly.
- **Atroxian Realm → The Alphamatic:**
  [Gridlock's release announcement](https://pckf.com/viewtopic.php?t=4086)
  identifies The Alphamatic as the continuation of Atroxian Realm.
- **Aurora Bliss's epilogue → Canmore's Redemption:**
  [Dave216's announcement](https://pckf.com/viewtopic.php?t=16501)
  explicitly identifies the sequel relationship.
- **Bounty → Prisoner → Clorts:**
  [Kohntarkosz_'s Clorts announcement](https://pckf.com/viewtopic.php?t=11633)
  gives the trilogy's order.
- **Suburb after the universe-saving adventure:**
  [KeenWiki's transcription of its story](https://keenwiki.shikadi.net/wiki/Suburb_Shenanigans)
  supplies this anchor; confirm against the selected game's actual story
  files before implementing a strict unlock.
- **Marooned on Mars — Galaxy as a retelling:**
  [Dr.Colossus's announcement](https://pckf.com/viewtopic.php?t=3598)
  identifies it as a Keen 5 redesign of the first game.
