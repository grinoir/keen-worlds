KEEN'S ARCADE
============

Walk to the cabinet beside the ship's bunk and press Up. It offers the
original Commander Keen 1, 2 and 3. Under Keen 1 choose Original or the
Galaxy remake, Marooned on Mars - Galaxy by Dr. Colossus (2020 fixes by
K1n9_Duk3). Both versions are playable natively.

Supply your own games, using v1.31 for the originals:

  arcade/keen1/   Marooned on Mars
  arcade/keen2/   The Earth Explodes
  arcade/keen3/   Keen Must Die!
  arcade/mars/    Marooned on Mars - Galaxy (converted data)

Include each game's KEEN1.EXE / KEEN2.EXE / KEEN3.EXE and all its .CK1 /
.CK2 / .CK3 files. Filenames are matched regardless of case. Other versions
and Vorticons mods have not been integrated. Missing or unsupported games
show an explanation in the cabinet.

For the Galaxy remake, unpack the K1n9_Duk3-fixed archive dated September
2020, then convert your copy once with the source tools:

  python3 kwtools/kw_mars.py MOD_FOLDER KEEN5E.EXE OUTPUT_FOLDER

Use your original Keen 5 v1.4 KEEN5E.EXE and arcade/mars as OUTPUT_FOLDER.
The executable is read only to recover its patched tables, never run.
The converter checks the supported patch revision and all sprite actions.
It installs the remake's maps, graphics, audio and MARS.CK5. The original
Keen 1 files stay in arcade/keen1; the remake's data stays in arcade/mars.

The originals run natively with Chocolate Keen, by NY00123, QuantumG, Lemm, James
Mackenzie and contributors. The DOS programs are read for their data; they
are not executed. No DOSBox is needed. The native keen-vorticons program
must be installed beside the Keen Worlds program.
The Galaxy remake runs inside the current Keen Worlds engine and window.
It starts on Mars with no ammunition and must find its pogo stick. Its
progress is included in the main save slots; its score, lives, ammunition,
items and difficulty remain separate from the wider journey.

The originals open their own window. Exit to return to the cabinet and your
unchanged cabin. Use its original controls (Arrows, Ctrl to jump, Alt to
pogo, Ctrl+Alt to fire; F3 redefines the keys). F5 saves on the world map.
Screen mode follows the visible Keen Worlds window, including desktop
fullscreen shortcuts. Fullscreen stays fullscreen on return. In the Galaxy
remake press Escape and choose Return to cabin, Save Game or Load Game.

Each game's saves, scores, controls and settings stay in its own folder
under the Keen Worlds user path: arcade/keen1, arcade/keen2 or arcade/keen3.
The remake uses the main save slots and settings. Return to Cabin leaves
Keen beside the cabinet; New Game restarts the remake, and End Game or
running out of lives returns to the cabin. Completing it also returns there.
Old saves from its earlier separate player remain in arcade/mars.
In a normal portable installation those are the folders listed above.
Keep them when updating. Lives, score, ammunition and items do not transfer
to or from Keen Worlds. Arcade completion never unlocks planets or counts
towards the all-worlds-saved ending.

The original games are by id Software. No original arcade game data is
included in this release. The native engine's GPL license and origin are
in COPYING.txt and ENGINE.txt; its matching source is in source.tar.gz.

The cabinet's CRT has moving/blinking pixels. Entry and return fade through
black, with fullscreen preserved. When an original game asks where to exit,
choose (A)rcade to return; the old (D) key still works. Its DOS information
screen is omitted. Keen Dreams uses longer fades on falling asleep/waking.

The cabinet introductions use Keen's established bm_fable voice at speed 1.2.
Voice mute/volume still apply; ordinary fresh presses advance the popups.
