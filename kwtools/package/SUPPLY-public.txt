1. What is in it, and what you can add
--------------------------------------

Keen 4 is in it. Its shareware episode, Secret of the Oracle, is free:
id Software and Apogee let anyone pass it on. The whole shareware
package, unchanged, is in the folder keen4-shareware (its terms are in
VENDOR.DOC there), and its three game files are also next to the
program. So you can start right away.

Keen 5, Keen 6 and the fan games are not in it: they are their owners'.
Add the ones you have, like this:

  KeenWorlds folder
    GAMEMAPS.CK4  EGAGRAPH.CK4  AUDIO.CK4    Keen 4 (already here)
    GAMEMAPS.CK5  EGAGRAPH.CK5  AUDIO.CK5    copy from your Keen 5
    GAMEMAPS.CK6  EGAGRAPH.CK6  AUDIO.CK6    copy from your Keen 6
    mods/keen7, mods/bounty, ...            unpack each fan game into
                                             its folder (mods/README.txt)

Keen 5 and 6 must be version 1.4 (EGA), from your own copy. Copy only
those three files of each; the other .CK5 and .CK6 files here belong to
Keen Worlds.

If your Keen 6 is version 1.5, copy the files from the keen6-v1.5 folder
over the ones here first. Other versions of Keen 6 do not fit; the program
says so when it starts instead of running.

Anything missing shows as "not installed" in the ship's flight menu, and
choosing it there says where it comes from. Four fan worlds need only
Keen 4, so they work with what is here once their game is unpacked:
Krodacia (keen7), Aspermontis (keen95), Meurglys III (bounty) and the
dream, Keen Dreams (dreams).
