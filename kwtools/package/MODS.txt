THE FAN GAMES
=============

Each fan game is a world of its own in the flight menu. Keen Worlds does
not contain them: they are their authors' work. A world whose files are
missing shows as "not installed", and choosing it in the flight menu
tells what it is and where it comes from.

One folder for each world goes here:

  keen7    Krodacia             The Keys of Krodacia
  keen8    Calidune             Dead in the Desert
  keen9    HUB Command Center   Battle of the Brains
  keen95   Aspermontis          The Eight Accumulators
  bounty   Meurglys III         Bounty Hunting Brouhaha
  ck55     The Omegamatic II    Keen 5.5
  asylum   Address Unknown      Asylum
  osi      The Shikadi Station  Operation Station Infiltration
  arealm   Krisle               Atroxian Realm
  fitf     Gnosticus Pi         Foray in the Forest
  dreams   Tuberia              Keen Dreams

Most of these games were not made for this engine. Their files have to be
converted first, with the tools in the source (kwtools/). There is no
one-step setup for that yet; until there is, ask the person who gave you
this test version for the converted folders.
