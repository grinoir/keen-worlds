THE FAN GAMES
=============

Each fan game is a world of its own in the ship's flight menu. Keen Worlds
does not contain them: they are their authors' work. You download a game
and unpack it into its folder here. That is all.

  - The folders are already here. The few files in them belong to Keen
    Worlds; leave them.
  - Unpack the download as it is. If it makes a folder of its own inside,
    that is fine.
  - A world whose game is missing shows as "not installed" in the flight
    menu. Choose it there to read where it comes from.
  - Start Keen Worlds again after adding a game.

Where each game comes from:

  keen7    Krodacia             The Keys of Krodacia, by Ceilick
  ck55     The Omegamatic II    Keen 5.5, by The CK Guy
  asylum   Address Unknown      Asylum
           All three: the Omnispeak modding kit (github.com/sulix/omnispeak,
           release "1.2 preview"). In it, the folders data/mod/keen7,
           data/mod/ck55 and data/mod/asylum.

  keen8    Calidune             Dead in the Desert, by Ceilick (2009)
  keen9    HUB Command Center   Battle of the Brains, by Ceilick, Mink and
                                Tulip (2011)
           Both: archive.org, search "Commander Keen 8 - Dead in the Desert"
           and "Commander Keen 9 - Battle of the Brains".

  keen95   Aspermontis          The Eight Accumulators, by Bubbatom (2011)
  bounty   Meurglys III         Bounty Hunting Brouhaha v1.0, by
                                Kohntarkosz_ (2019)
  osi      The Shikadi Station  Operation Station Infiltration v1.4, by
                                Ceilick (2022): the Omnispeak version
  fitf     Gnosticus Pi         Foray in the Forest v2.0, by Nisaba and
                                K1n9_Duk3
           All four: their pages on keenwiki.shikadi.net.

  arealm   Krisle               Atroxian Realm v1.5, by Gridlock (2016)
           Its thread on pckf.com.

  dreams   Tuberia              Keen Dreams, shareware version 1.13
           Free to pass on; many DOS archives have it (archive.org: search
           "Keen Dreams shareware 1.13"). Four of its files are needed:
           KDREAMS.EXE (81619 bytes), KDREAMS.MAP, .EGA and .AUD. Keen
           Worlds makes its own .KDR files from them when it starts; you
           never need to find those. Other versions (the 2015 Steam
           release, the Definitive Edition) do not work. If Keen can't
           sleep, the bed says what is missing. This is the dream Keen has
           in the ship's bed, not a place in the flight menu.

  rtts     Far Shadowlands      Return to the Shadowlands v1.8, by Ceilick
                                (programming K1n9_Duk3), 2022-12-21
           From its KeenWiki page (l.ceilick.return_to_the_shadowlands
           _v1.8...zip). Unpack the whole zip in mods/rtts, beside the
           RTTS.CK4 and AUDINFOE.RTT that come with Keen Worlds. Other
           versions are refused (the converter checks the v1.8 files).

  dreams-gold  (the bunk)       Keen Dreams Gold Edition, by Szemigi
           Release of 2026-05-31: KDGOLD.ZIP from its thread,
           https://pckf.com/viewtopic.php?t=18318. Unpack the whole zip in
           mods/dreams-gold, keeping its KDGSRC folder (three header files
           are read from it). Gold runs in its own native program
           (keen-dreams-gold), like the arcade's games: the bunk asks which
           dream, and Gold keeps its own saves and settings, apart from the
           journey. Other Gold releases are refused by name and size.

Each world needs its base game too: Keen 4 for keen7, keen95, bounty and
dreams; Keen 5 for keen9, ck55, asylum, osi, arealm, alphamatic and fitf; Keen 6 for
keen8.

  alphamatic  The Alphamatic    The Alphamatic v3.0, by Gridlock
              https://pckf.com/viewtopic.php?t=4086
              Unpack the original mod archive in mods/alphamatic.
              Continues Atroxian Realm; unlocked after it in the Chronicle.
